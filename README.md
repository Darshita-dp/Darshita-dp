## About

I am a **Data & Software Analyst** building decision-support systems for healthcare and supply-chain operations.

My work connects **SQL data models, Python pipelines, Power BI dashboards, machine learning, FastAPI services, React interfaces, and workflow automation**. I focus on converting operational data into clear actions—what should be prioritized, escalated, corrected, scheduled, or investigated next.

I also translate technical findings into practical recommendations for both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

**Target roles:** `Data Analyst` · `Healthcare Data Analyst` · `BI Analyst` · `Power BI Developer` · `Business Systems Analyst`

## Featured Decision Systems

### Healthcare Revenue Cycle Command Center

> End-to-end revenue-cycle decision-support platform that helps teams identify high-priority claims, analyze denial patterns, evaluate payer performance and accelerate revenue recovery.

- Claim denial, payer-performance and A/R aging analytics
- Explainable 0–100 claim-priority scoring
- Revenue-recovery simulation and escalation logic
- React operational work queue and Power BI reporting
- API-driven architecture with containerized deployment support

**Stack:** `Python` `SQL` `PostgreSQL` `FastAPI` `React` `TypeScript` `Power BI` `DAX` `Docker`

[View Repository →](https://github.com/Darshita-dp/Healthcare-Revenue-Cycle-Command-Center)

---

### Patient Access & Appointment No-Show Automation

> Healthcare scheduling platform that predicts no-show risk, recommends operational actions and helps clinics recover unused appointment capacity.

- 0.72 ROC-AUC on a temporal holdout dataset
- Risk-based staff recommendations and outreach actions
- Waitlist matching for released and available appointment slots
- Provider, clinic-utilization and management views
- Fairness and subgroup-performance audit documentation

**Stack:** `Python` `Scikit-learn` `PostgreSQL` `FastAPI` `React` `Power BI` `Power Automate`

[View Repository →](https://github.com/Darshita-dp/Patient-Access-No-Show-Automation-Platform)

---

### AI-Powered Supply-Chain Data Quality & BOM Remediation

> Enterprise-data platform designed to detect BOM quality failures, resolve conflicting part records and prioritize high-risk remediation work.

- Validation rules for duplicates, missing attributes and broken relationships
- Python-based authoritative-record scoring
- Data-quality exception prioritization and remediation workflows
- Supply-chain reporting for BOM, inventory, cost and lead-time records
- AI-assisted investigation and root-cause support

**Stack:** `Python` `SQL` `Snowflake` `Power BI` `Data Quality` `AI`

[View Repository →](https://github.com/Darshita-dp/AI-Powered-Supply-Chain-Data-Quality-and-BOM-Remediation-Platform)
